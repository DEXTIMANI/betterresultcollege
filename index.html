About the Director

Mr. Fab S. Chukwu

Mr. Fab S. Chukwu is the Director and Founder of Better Result Christian Schools. He hails from Ihuokpara in Nkanu East Local Government Area of Enugu State.

He holds a Nigeria Certificate in Education (N.C.E) in Education/Social Studies from the National Teachers’ Institute, Kaduna, and a Bachelor’s Degree in Education (B.Ed) in Environmental Education/Social Studies from the University of Calabar, Cross River State.

Driven by a strong passion for quality education and moral upbringing, he established Better Result Christian Schools as a government-approved institution committed to academic excellence, character development, and holistic learning at the Nursery, Primary, and Secondary levels.
