About the Director

Mr. Fab S. Chukwu

Mr. Fab S. Chukwu is the Director and Founder of Better Result Christian Schools. He hails from Ihuokpara in Nkanu East Local Government Area of Enugu State.

He holds a Nigeria Certificate in Education (N.C.E) in Education/Social Studies from the National Teachers’ Institute, Kaduna, and a Bachelor’s Degree in Education (B.Ed) in Environmental Education/Social Studies from the University of Calabar, Cross River State.

Driven by a strong passion for quality education and moral upbringing, he established Better Result Christian Schools as a government-approved institution committed to academic excellence, character development, and holistic learning at the Nursery, Primary, and Secondary levels.

Better Results College: Nurturing Excellence Since 1993

Established in 1993, Better Results College is a beacon of academic excellence, empowering students to achieve their full potential. Our institution is renowned for guiding students to success in key examinations, including WAEC and NECO exams.

At Better Results College, we don't just educate; we transform lives. Our alumni are making waves in various fields, with accomplished professionals in medicine, law, engineering, and beyond.

We are committed to fostering a culture of excellence, integrity, and faith, shaping leaders who impact their communities and the world.

Academics at Better Result College

Our academic programme is structured to develop intellectual excellence, discipline, confidence, and strong moral values through quality teaching and continuous assessment.
